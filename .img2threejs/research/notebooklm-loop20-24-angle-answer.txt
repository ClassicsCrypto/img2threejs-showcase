The Loop 20 audit of the Three.js AWP | Medusa reconstruction confirms a successful restoration of the bolt handle proportions, but the model requires further local geometric refinement to meet Tier-1 silhouette and symmetry thresholds. ### 1. Macro Receiver, Stock, and Barrel Profile
*   **OBSERVED:** The procedural renders (Angles 01–24) confirm the receiver and stock still exhibit a "blocky" read compared to the tapered, organic transitions seen in the origin front/back images [2, 3].
*   **SUPPORTED:** Technical specifications for the Accuracy International AWP/AW series define an overall length of 1120mm with a 24" barrel [4, 5]. The current `aspectRatioDelta` suggests the model is slightly compressed or over-volumed in the Y-axis.
*   **REFINE PLAN:** Rather than uniform thinning (rejected in Loop 17), implement a non-linear taper on the receiver’s upper shoulders and the stock’s thumbhole-to-buttpad transition to better match the origin silhouette [1, 3].

### 2. Scope, Rings, and Mount Physical Contact

*   **INFERRED:** The current overlap is visually sufficient for a game-item reconstruction, though the ring fasteners (screws) lack the distinct hex-head depth seen in technical diagrams [6, 11].

### 3. Crown and Medusa Decal Integrity
